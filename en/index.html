Zikirmatik — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Zikirmatik collects no data about your use of the app and sends nothing to any server.

What is stored

The daily counts of the dhikr you recite, the dhikr you add and your app settings are kept only in your device's own storage.

No account needed

You do not need to create an account or provide an email address or phone number to use the app.

Third parties

This version contains no advertising network, analytics or crash reporting service.

Deleting your data

When you remove the app from your device, all of your data is deleted with it.

Contact

For questions or requests about this privacy policy: zikirmatik.destek@gmail.com

This app currently contains no advertising network, analytics or crash reporting; this policy will be updated when advertising is added.
