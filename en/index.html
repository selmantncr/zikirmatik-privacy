Zikirmatik — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Your dhikr records stay on your device; Zikirmatik sends them to no server. The app is free and shows ads — these are served by Google AdMob, which processes the limited data described below.

What is stored

The daily counts of the dhikr you recite, the dhikr you add and your app settings are kept only in your device's own storage. This data is not sent to us or to any other party, and it is never shared with the advertising network.

No account needed

You do not need to create an account or provide an email address or phone number to use the app.

Advertising

Ads in the app are served by Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and protect ads against fraud, AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier, an approximate location derived from your IP address (country or city level), device and operating system information, and your ad interactions (impressions and clicks).

Ad content is kept to the narrowest age range: only ads rated "G" (suitable for everyone) are requested.

You can read how Google processes this data at Google Privacy & Terms.

Your choices

iOS: The first time you open the app, you are asked whether to allow tracking. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised. You can change your mind later under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Android: You can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent form when the app first opens; you set your preference there.

Deleting your data

When you remove the app from your device, all of the data kept on the device is deleted with it.

Contact

For questions or requests about this privacy policy: destek.zikirmatik@outlook.com

The app contains no analytics or crash reporting service. Apart from serving ads, no data is collected.
